# Code of Conduct

The company has one rule: **be the colleague you'd want to work with.**

Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person. Keep disagreements technical.
Remember everyone here is doing this for free, on their own time.

Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language get you fired: the maintainer may
edit, delete or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this rule, and
may block repeat offenders.

Report anything serious via a security advisory or directly to the maintainer.
Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
